# Kalakal product audit — 14 Nov 2026 (v0.8 → v0.9)

Lens: gamified products that keep people coming back (Duolingo-style): fast first win, clear feedback,
many small wins, a reason to return, and a loop that brings new people in.

## What already works well
- Join in seconds with a PIN, no account. Characters, fun names, streaks, milestones, confetti, haptics.
- Speed + streak scoring, double and warm-up rounds, host highlights on the scoreboard.
- Five quick ways to create (AI, web link, paste, spreadsheet, scratch) in a three-step builder.
- Library with likes, reports, review queue. Security headers, tests for every area.

## Fixed in v0.9 (this release)
| # | Gap | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Players only saw rank and points; the bottom half had nothing to aim for | Result screen says how fast you answered, the streak bonus, places gained, and who to chase ("Only 120 pts behind Malli") or the size of your lead |
| 2 | Losing a streak was silent | "Your streak of 4 ended. Start a new one!" |
| 3 | Only three people won | Awards for games with 4+ players: Fastest fingers, Streak master, Sharpshooter (one per player), on the big screen and on the winner's phone; "Fastest right answer" each question |
| 4 | Final phone screen was a dead end | Personal stats (right answers, best streak, average speed) and a link to the library |
| 5 | No "one more game" loop | Play again on the podium: same quiz, new PIN, and every phone gets a Join the rematch button |

## Backlog, in order of value
1. **Sound** on the big screen (lobby music, countdown ticks, reveal sting) with a mute button. Biggest missing piece of "game feel".
2. **Creator loop:** after a game, e-mail-free recap on My quizzes (plays, average score, hardest question) and a "host streak" for weekly hosts.
3. **Question report after the game:** which questions most people got wrong; one tap to fix them.
4. **Player identity across games** (optional, no account): keep your character and name on the device, show "games played" and badges.
5. **Team mode** and **self-paced mode** (share a link, play alone, leaderboard over a week).
6. **Home page:** a 10-second demo loop and one sample question you can answer before any sign-up.
7. **Library ranking:** show "played by N groups" and completion rate; featured row picked by admin.
8. **Accessibility pass:** screen-reader labels on answer tiles, reduced-motion check on result screens, colour-blind check of red/green in the outline.
9. **Rate limiting** on game creation and joins (Turnstile on create when traffic grows).
10. **Rematch keeps the saved-quiz link** so repeat plays count in the library.

## Known limits of this audit
- Reviewed code and the local build; the live site, real Google sign-in and reading real web links were not exercised.
- The awards row on the big screen is covered by automated tests but was not checked by eye with real award data.
