Way 1 · Fastest

Type or paste your questions

Write the quiz as plain text: the question, then the answers. A star marks the right one. Kalakal shows a live preview as you type.

  1. Open the quiz builder and press Paste or type questions.
  2. Type a question, then each answer on its own line. Put * before the right answer and - before the others.
  3. Leave one empty line before the next question.
  4. Check the preview on the right and press Add to quiz.

Best for: quizzes you already have in Word, Notes, WhatsApp or an email.

Way 2

Build it question by question

The classic way, made quick: you can do almost everything from the keyboard.

  1. Type the question and press Enter to jump to the answers.
  2. Type each answer, pressing Enter after each one. Enter on the last answer starts a new question.
  3. Tap Right on the correct answer. Tap more than one if several answers are right.
  4. Choose the time and the points: Normal, Double for a bonus round, or No points for a warm-up.
  5. Press Create game. The PIN and QR code appear for your players.

Best for: new quizzes, and fine-tuning questions you pasted or imported.

Way 3

Bring a spreadsheet

Keep your question bank in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or LibreOffice and bring it in whenever you need it.

  1. Download the template and open it in your spreadsheet app.
  2. One question per row. In Correct write the number of the right answer (1–4), or several like 1,3.
  3. Select the rows, copy, and paste them into Paste or type questions. Or save as CSV and use Import spreadsheet.
  4. To keep a copy or share it, use Export as spreadsheet.

Best for: teachers, big question banks, and teams who write quizzes together in a shared sheet.

Way 4 · New

Create it with AI

Say what the quiz is about, or give it your own notes or a PDF. Kalakal's AI writes the questions, you check them and add the ones you like.

  1. In the quiz builder, press Create with AI.
  2. Write a topic, like "the solar system" or "Pongal festival". Or open use your own notes and paste text or pick a PDF: the AI then only asks about what's in it.
  3. Choose the number of questions, difficulty, who's playing and the language (English, Tamil, Swedish and more).
  4. Check every answer. Tap New question, Harder, Easier or Better wrong answers to fix one, or untick it.
  5. Press Add to quiz. The questions work like any others: edit them, add pictures, play.

Free: 5 AI quizzes a day per person. Prefer ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini? Use Copy AI prompt in the paste window and paste the reply back.

Way 5

Add pictures, GIFs and videos

Make questions visual: guess the place, name the actor, or play a clip and ask what happens next.

  1. On a quiz question, press Add picture. You can also drag a picture onto the question, or paste one with Ctrl/Cmd + V.
  2. For a GIF or an online picture, paste its link into the link box.
  3. For a video, paste a YouTube link and set the start and end time, so only the part you want plays.
  4. The picture or video shows on the big screen. Phones just show the answer buttons, so they stay fast on busy Wi-Fi.

Good to know: big photos are shrunk automatically. Use pictures you took yourself or are allowed to use.

Questions people ask

Do I need an account?

No. Your quiz is saved as a draft in this browser while you work. To keep a copy, move it to another computer or share it with a friend, press Export as spreadsheet, then import it there. Saved quiz libraries with sign-in are coming.

Where is my quiz saved, and can I lose it?

Drafts are stored in your browser on this device. They stay when you close the tab, but clearing your browser data or using a private window removes them. Export important quizzes as a spreadsheet to be safe.

How many questions and answers can a quiz have?

Up to 50 slides per session. Each quiz question has 2 to 4 answers and 10 to 60 seconds to answer. Questions can be up to 200 characters and answers up to 80.

Can more than one answer be right?

Yes. Tap Right on every correct answer, or use * on several lines. Players pick one answer, and any of the right ones scores. The big screen highlights all of them at the reveal.

How do points work? What are Double and No points?

A right answer scores 500 to 1,000 points: the faster, the more. Answering in a row adds a streak bonus of up to +500. Double doubles the speed points, which is great for a final round. No points makes a warm-up question: players answer and see if they were right, but scores and streaks don't change.

What can I add: pictures, GIFs, videos?

Pictures: upload, drag or paste JPG, PNG or WebP. Big photos are shrunk automatically so they load fast.

GIFs: upload one up to 1.5 MB, or paste a link to a GIF online.

Videos: paste a YouTube link and choose the start and end. Uploading your own video files isn't possible yet.

Pictures and videos show on the big screen. Phones show the answer buttons, which keeps the game fast for everyone.

Can AI write my quiz?

Yes. In the builder press Create with AI, write a topic (or paste notes or pick a PDF), choose the number of questions, difficulty, audience and language, and press Write my quiz. You'll see every question before anything is added.

Always check the answers. AI can be confidently wrong, especially on recent events, local facts and numbers. Use New question, Harder, Easier or Better wrong answers to fix a question, or untick it.

How many AI quizzes can I make?

5 AI quizzes a day per person, each up to 20 questions, plus 40 single-question changes. The count resets every night at 1 am Swedish time (2 am in summer). If you run out, use Copy AI prompt in the paste window with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It's unlimited and the reply pastes straight back in.

Can the AI write in Tamil or Swedish?

Yes. Pick the language before you press Write my quiz: English, Tamil, Swedish, Hindi and several more, or type any other language under Other…. Quality is best in English and widely used languages; in Tamil, read the questions carefully and fix any odd wording in the editor.

Can the AI make a quiz from my own notes or a PDF?

Yes. Open Or use your own notes, an article or a PDF, paste the text or pick a PDF or .txt file. The AI then only asks about what's in your text, which is great for lessons, training and company onboarding. It reads up to about 12,000 characters (roughly 5 pages). Scanned PDFs without real text can't be read: copy the text instead. Word and PowerPoint: copy the text and paste it.

What happens to my notes and topics? Is my data used for training?

Your topic and notes are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the questions, and aren't stored by Kalakal. Cloudflare doesn't use them to train models. Don't paste personal or confidential information you wouldn't want processed by an AI service.

Which spreadsheet apps work?

Any of them: Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers or LibreOffice. The quickest way is to select the rows, copy and paste them into Paste or type questions. To import a file, save it as CSV first (in Google Sheets: File → Download → CSV). The column guide lists what goes where.

Can I write questions in Tamil, Swedish or other languages?

Yes, any language, and you can mix languages in one quiz. Tamil script is fully supported on the big screen and on phones.

Can I change questions or right answers after creating the game?

Yes, as long as the game hasn't started. In the lobby press Edit questions: you get the full editor, where you can change the wording, tap Right on a different answer, add or remove questions. Press Save and back to lobby; the PIN stays the same and players who already joined keep waiting. AI questions can also be edited in the review window before you add them: tap an answer to mark it right, or press Edit.

How do I invite people to join?

The lobby shows the PIN and a QR code for people in the room. For groups and chats, use WhatsApp, Copy link or Share invite (on phones and Macs) in the lobby. The link opens the game with the PIN already filled in, so people only type their name.

Can I mix polls, word clouds and Q&A into a quiz?

Yes. In the builder, under Add a slide, you can add polls, word clouds, open questions, 1–5 scales and Q&A between quiz questions. They don't affect the scores.

How many people can play?

Up to 200 players per game. They join on their phones with the PIN or QR code. No app needed.

Is it free?

Yes. Creating and hosting quizzes is free. Extras for schools, companies and big events may come later; the free version stays.

Something isn't working. What should I check?

"Needs at least 2 answers": every question needs two or more answers, each on its own line.

"Say which answer is right": mark one with *, tap Right, or fill the Correct column.

"Too big to save in this browser": you've uploaded many large pictures. Use picture links for some questions instead.

Excel file won't import: save it as CSV first, or copy and paste the rows.
