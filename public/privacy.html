Privacy policy

Kalakal is a free quiz and audience-engagement app. We collect as little as we can, never sell your data and don't use tracking or advertising cookies. This policy explains what we store, why, and your rights under the GDPR.

Who is responsible

Kalakal is run by Nexvik Consulting (Prabhakaran Muruganantham), Stockholm, Sweden, the data controller. Contact: hello@kalakal.se.

Players (no account)

To join a game you only type a nickname. During a game we keep your nickname, character, answers and score in memory so the game works. Game data is deleted within about 6 hours. Your browser remembers your nickname and character on your device; you can clear it any time.

Create with AI

When you use Create with AI, the topic, settings and any notes or PDF text you add are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the questions. Kalakal doesn't store that text. PDFs are read in your browser; only the extracted text is sent. Please don't include personal or confidential information.

To share the free daily AI allowance fairly, we keep a scrambled code made from your internet address. It changes every day and can't be turned back into the address.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare hosts Kalakal, its database, picture storage and AI (data processor; EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply to transfers).
  • Google provides the sign-in, under Google's own privacy policy.

We don't sell or share personal data with anyone else, and we don't use analytics that track you across sites.

Public content

Quizzes you make Public are shown in the library with your display name. Unlisted quizzes can be seen by anyone with the link. You can change this or delete a quiz at any time.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Anyone can join a game as a player without an account and without giving personal data. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

  • See and download your data: My quizzes → Account → Download my data.
  • Delete everything: My quizzes → Account → Delete my account. This removes your account, quizzes, pictures and likes immediately.
  • Correct, restrict or object to processing, or ask questions: hello@kalakal.se.
  • You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in cookies can't be read by scripts. Only the people who run Kalakal can open the admin tools, and only to keep the library safe.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll show a notice on the site before it applies.
